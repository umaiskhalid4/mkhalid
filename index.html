The Works

Projects

01

BIP

Offline-First Personal Finance PWA · AI-Directed Development

Solves a specific budgeting gap: biweekly income doesn't map cleanly onto calendar-month bills, and most tools get it wrong. BIP tracks spending by calendar month while correctly detecting which months carry two paycheques versus three, calculates take-home pay from raw hours using a reverse-engineered deduction model (CPP, EI, income tax, RRSP, benefit premiums), and automatically reconciles recurring bills against actual bank and card transactions. Multi-format CSV statement import handles inconsistent date formats and sign conventions, with a learning rule system that auto-categorizes transactions over time. Runs entirely client-side — no backend, no account, no ongoing cost — with data persisted via localStorage and full offline support through a versioned service worker, installable straight to an iOS home screen. Directed the product design and financial logic; build executed in partnership with Claude.
